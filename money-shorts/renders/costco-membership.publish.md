# How Costco ACTUALLY makes money 🛒

## Caption / description

Costco sold $297B last year but kept only ~$3 of every $100 as profit. The real engine is membership fees: $5.9B, equal to ~64% of its profit. All figures from Costco's FY2026 results (fiscal year ended Aug 30, 2026). Sources below.

**[hook]** Over the past year, Costco sold $297 billion worth of stuff. But that's not really how it makes money.
**[thin-profit]** Out of $303 billion in total revenue, Costco kept just $9.2 billion as profit.

**[fees-vs-profit]** That's equal to about 64% of Costco's entire profit, before it sells you a single thing.
**[renewals]** And people keep paying. 92.3% of members in the US and Canada renew.
**[hot-dog]** It's why the $1.50 hot dog hasn't changed price in over 40 years. When an executive suggested raising it, co-founder Jim Sinegal reportedly said, if you raise the effing hot dog, I will kill you.
**[reframe]** Costco isn't really selling you groceries. It's selling you the membership.
