## Glossary (language examples)

- **estoy aburrido** (es) = I'm bored · sources: kwiziq_meaning, coffeebreak
- **soy aburrido** (es) = I'm boring · sources: kwiziq_meaning, coffeebreak
- **ser** (es) = to be (identity, traits, origin) · sources: spanishdict_guide
- **estar** (es) = to be (states, conditions, location) · sources: spanishdict_guide
- **es listo** (es) = he's smart · sources: kwiziq_meaning, coffeebreak
- **está listo** (es) = he's ready · sources: kwiziq_meaning, coffeebreak
- **es rico** (es) = he's rich · sources: kwiziq_meaning, coffeebreak
- **está rico** (es) = it's delicious (food) · sources: kwiziq_meaning, coffeebreak
- **es malo** (es) = he's a bad person · sources: kwiziq_meaning, coffeebreak
- **está malo** (es) = he's sick (also: food that tastes bad or has gone off) · sources: kwiziq_meaning, coffeebreak — common in Spain; 'está enfermo' is the neutral way to say sick
- **es vivo** (es) = he's lively / sharp · sources: kwiziq_meaning
- **está vivo** (es) = he's alive · sources: kwiziq_meaning
- **está muerto** (es) = he's dead · sources: rae_muerto, cvc_muerto — estar is used even though death is permanent
- **la fiesta es en mi casa** (es) = the party is at my house · sources: spanishdict_events, kwiziq_events — events 'take place'
- **el café está frío** (es) = the coffee is cold · sources: spanishdict_guide — a changed condition
